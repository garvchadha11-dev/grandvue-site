Welcome to GVA

Your New Home in Arusha

Grand Vue Apartments is a modern residential community in Arusha, Tanzania. Quietly and securely set at the heart of Olorien, it is framed by lush gardens and the breathtaking scenery of Mt. Meru and Mt. Kilimanjaro.

GVA comprises 55 apartments for rent, with one, two and three-bedroom layouts, available furnished or unfurnished, perfectly suited to you and your family. It is a stylish way of living, bringing together amenities elegantly composed with a modern, contemporary influence.

We don't settle for less. Whether you are relocating, posted to Arusha or simply ready for a better address, GVA is where heaven meets earth.

About Arusha

The Heart of Northern Tanzania

Set on the southern slopes of Mt. Meru at 1,400 metres, Arusha enjoys cool, dry mountain air year-round. It is the headquarters of the East African Community and the gateway to the Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater and Mt. Kilimanjaro.

A cosmopolitan, fast-growing city, and the perfect place to call home.
